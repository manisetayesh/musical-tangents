How we calculate it:

Popularity ≈ 18.2 × Danceability + (-6.5) × Energy + 0.98 × Loudness + (-2.7) × Speechiness + (-10.4) × Acousticness + (-9.2) × Instrumentalness + (-11.3) × Liveness + (-12.8) × Valence

Calculated via scikit-learn LinearRegression. Coefficients represent the weight of each standardized feature.
